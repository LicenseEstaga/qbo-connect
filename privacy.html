Privacy Policy

Estaga Accounting Assistant · Effective October 27, 2026

Estaga Accounting Assistant (the "App") is a private, internal tool operated by Estaga (estaga.com). It connects only to Estaga's own QuickBooks Online company and is used only by authorized Estaga personnel. It is not offered, sold or made available to any other business or individual. This policy explains how the App handles QuickBooks data.

Internal use only
  • QuickBooks data accessed by the App belongs to Estaga and is used only inside Estaga, for Estaga's own accounting.
  • It is never sold, rented, shared, published, disclosed or transferred to anyone outside Estaga.
  • It is never combined with other companies' data, used for marketing or advertising, or used to build or improve any product or service for others.
  • No one outside Estaga can use the App or see the data. The only exception is the AI service provider described below, which processes data solely to carry out a task requested by Estaga staff.

Data the App accesses

With the QuickBooks Online Accounting permission (com.intuit.quickbooks.accounting), the App can access Estaga's accounting data: company information, chart of accounts, vendors, customers, products and services, transactions (such as bills, expenses, invoices, payments and journal entries) and financial reports. The App does not request Payroll or Payments permissions.

How the data is used

  • To help Estaga staff with bookkeeping: reviewing reports, reconciling accounts and preparing entries such as bills and journal entries from Estaga's own source documents.
  • Every change to QuickBooks (create, update or delete) is shown to a staff member as a preview and is sent only after that person explicitly approves it.
  • Data is used solely for Estaga's own benefit and never for any purpose unrelated to Estaga's accounting.

AI processing

The App uses Claude, an AI model provided by Anthropic, as an assistant for Estaga staff. Only the QuickBooks data needed for a specific task requested by a staff member is sent to Anthropic, which processes it on Estaga's behalf solely to perform that task. Estaga's Anthropic account is configured so that this data is not used to train AI models. Anthropic does not receive access to Estaga's QuickBooks company itself; it only sees the data included in a request.

Sharing

Estaga does not share QuickBooks data obtained through the App with any third party. Apart from the AI processing described above, data is disclosed outside Estaga only if Estaga is required to do so by law.

Storage and security

  • The App has no server or database of its own. It runs on a company computer.
  • OAuth tokens and app credentials are stored locally on that computer, outside cloud-synced folders, and are never published or shared.
  • The App keeps a local log of API errors and of changes it makes (time, record ID, document number and Intuit's request ID) for troubleshooting and audit. Tokens and credentials are never written to the log.
  • All communication with Intuit uses HTTPS and OAuth 2.0.
  • The connection page at licenseestaga.github.io/qbo-connect only displays the address returned by Intuit so it can be copied into the App. It stores and sends nothing.

Retention and disconnecting

Local logs are kept only as long as needed for troubleshooting and audit, and are then deleted. Access can be removed at any time by disconnecting the App in QuickBooks Online (Apps → My Apps) or with the App's own disconnect function, which revokes the authorization at Intuit and deletes the locally stored tokens.

Changes and contact

Estaga may update this policy; the effective date above will change accordingly. Questions about this policy can be sent to Estaga using the contact details at estaga.com.
